# V3 — Windshield Dispatch

Reference: `Can't-Miss California National Parks Road Trip Itinerary.jfif`

Use case: stylized-concept  
Asset type: portrait 4:5 portfolio decision-system concept backdrop

Create a cinematic driver's-eye decision scene for OpenSeason. View a California mountain road through a windshield in clear late-afternoon light. A precise white route line appears as physical navigation ink across the view. Ahead, a blocked mountain-pass segment is marked once in safety red; a calm alternate route visibly bends toward a lower-elevation valley destination. Subtle wind barbs, heat hatching, precipitation dots and one fire-alert boundary stay attached to real terrain like a transportation dispatch overlay.

Use the reference only for its windshield perspective and route legibility. The result should feel documentary and operational, not futuristic. Use charcoal interior silhouette, sunlit granite and sage, white route ink and one red-orange hazard. No readable text, logos, dashboard grid, glass panels, neon, glow, floating 3D objects, unrelated abstraction, rainbow markers or watermark.
