Other decisions Ranking, alerts and recency

Ranking is not scoring.

Drive tolerance, interests, group profile, trip format and date modify the base fit score. The neutral group profile contributes zero to avoid double-counting group fit.

A closed road is not one fewer lane.

Alerts collapse into block, warn or inform. Old alerts expire after 180 days unless they are identified as standing orders.
