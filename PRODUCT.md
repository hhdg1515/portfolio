# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are prospective freelance and contract clients evaluating whether Clark Huang can independently take a digital product from research and design through implementation and delivery. They may be founders, small teams, agencies, or business owners who need one person capable of owning both product thinking and production code.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio exists to prove that Clark is an excellent freelance design-to-code practitioner. It should help a prospective client quickly understand the quality and range of his work, inspect credible project evidence, and decide to start a conversation by email.

The primary conversion is an email inquiry. LinkedIn is a secondary contact path.

## Positioning

Clark combines UX/UI design and full-stack implementation in one accountable freelance practice: he researches the problem, makes product and interface decisions, builds the system, and carries it through deployment or the documented project stage.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically arrive with limited time and evaluate the homepage before deciding whether to open individual case studies. They need to recognize the kind of work Clark does, distinguish live or built work from explorations and concepts, inspect evidence inside project pages, and find a direct email contact without navigating a sales funnel.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a static HTML/CSS portfolio with a small local Node preview server and no build step.
- The homepage presents six projects spanning automotive, travel, healthcare, fintech, consumer AI, and freight/customs.
- Project maturity language must remain factual and distinguish `exploration`, `concept`, `built`, `shipped`, `live`, and `production` accurately.
- Do not invent client names, testimonials, business outcomes, traffic, revenue, conversion improvements, delivery claims, or project metrics.
- Public case studies are the evidence layer. Private interview notes under `content/` are not public portfolio copy unless Clark explicitly promotes material from them.
- Email is the primary call to action.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name and personal identity: Clark Huang.
- The voice is direct, self-aware, technically credible, and specific about what was actually done.
- Preserve the core claim that design and code are performed by the same person, while proving it through project evidence rather than repeating the claim.
- Existing visual commitments are documented separately from product truth and must not be replaced without explicit redesign approval.

## Evidence on Hand

- Homepage project index: `index.html`.
- Six public project pages under `projects/`.
- Project cover assets under `assets/images/covers/`.
- Existing visual and editorial decision history: `../design-system.md`.
- Private interview-preparation material under `content/`.
- No blanket set of verified performance metrics or testimonials is currently available; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Show evidence before making claims.
2. Make project maturity and Clark's actual contribution explicit.
3. Let prospective clients reach strong work quickly with minimal interaction cost.
4. Use honest specificity to build trust; never inflate outcomes or delivery status.
5. End every credible evaluation path with an obvious email inquiry action.
